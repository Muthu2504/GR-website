FAQ

Delivery in Tirupur: your questions answered

Still not sure? Ask us on WhatsApp.

Do you do food delivery in Tirupur?

Yes. Groods delivers food from restaurants across Tirupur, including our partner restaurants like Junior Kuppanna, Sasi Briyani Hotel and The Biryani Theory. You can also ask us to pick up from any other restaurant of your choice. More about food delivery

Can I get medicines delivered at home in Tirupur?

Yes. We pick up prescription and everyday medicines from licensed medical shops in Tirupur and bring them to your door. More about medicine delivery

Do you offer parcel delivery within Tirupur?

Yes. We pick up and drop parcels, documents and packages anywhere across Tirupur city. Share the pickup and drop details and we will handle the rest. More about parcel delivery

Do you deliver groceries in Tirupur?

Yes. Send us your grocery list and we will buy your daily essentials and fresh items from the shop and deliver them to your home. More about grocery delivery

What are your delivery hours?

We deliver every day from 11:00 am to 3:30 pm and from 6:00 pm to 10:00 pm. You can message us outside these hours and we will reply as soon as we open.

How do I place an order?

Message us on WhatsApp or call 90 921 621 70. Tell us what you need along with the pickup and drop details. No app or sign-up is needed.

How much does delivery cost?

It depends on the distance and the order. We confirm the price with you before we pick anything up, so there are no surprises.

How can I pay?

You can pay online or pay on delivery.
